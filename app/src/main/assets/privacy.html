Privacy Policy

VEYLORA VPN · Your privacy. Your Freedom.
Effective date: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VEYLORA VPN (the “App”), developed by TK Sadhan (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the App. By using the App you agree to this policy.

In short: we do not collect, log, sell or share the websites you visit, the content of your traffic, or your DNS queries. The App has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

1. Information we do NOT collect

  • Browsing history, visited websites or apps
  • Contents of your network traffic
  • DNS queries
  • Your original IP address, connection timestamps or session duration on our servers
  • Personal identifiers such as your name, phone number or contacts (the App does not require an account)

2. Information handled on your device

  • Server selection and any WireGuard configuration files you import are stored only on your device, inside the App’s private storage. They are never uploaded to us.
  • The App uses Android’s VpnService API. This is required to create an encrypted tunnel and route your device’s traffic through a VPN server. Android shows its own permission dialog before the VPN starts.
  • The connection timer shown on the home screen is calculated locally on your device.

3. How the VPN works

When you connect, your traffic is encrypted using the WireGuard protocol and sent to a VPN server, which forwards it to the internet. To do this, the server necessarily sees the encrypted packets arriving from your IP address while you are connected. We do not record or retain this information. Websites you visit will see the VPN server’s IP address instead of yours.

4. Permissions used

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – to establish the VPN connection and detect network availability.
  • VPN service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE) – to create the VPN tunnel. The App uses this solely to provide the VPN feature you asked for. It does not use the VPN to monitor, modify, redirect or inject content into your traffic.

5. Third parties

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties and we do not include advertising or analytics SDKs. If you download the App from Google Play, Google may collect its own information under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not control that.

6. Data security

Traffic is protected with modern cryptography (WireGuard: Curve25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305, BLAKE2s). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by law in your country) and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain. You can delete all locally stored App data at any time by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App.

9. Legal requests

Since we do not keep activity logs, we have no browsing or connection history to provide if we receive a legal request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published with a new effective date inside the App and on this page.
